Kalends

A free, open-source wall calendar you run on your own computer or server. It merges your calendars into one dark, full-screen view for a spare display or a dashboard.

  • Google and Microsoft accounts: sign in and pick which calendars to show. Kalends only reads calendars.
  • Any iCal link: TeamSnap, SportsEngine, GameChanger, school calendars, iCloud, Sonarr, Radarr, holidays.
  • One colour per calendar, a big clock and date, today's agenda, a 5-day forecast and a multi-week month grid.
  • Reflows from a 4K portrait wall display to a small dashboard panel.

Your data stays with you

Kalends runs entirely on your own machine. There is no Kalends cloud service: calendar data goes straight from Google or Microsoft to your Kalends server and is never sent to the developer or anyone else. See the privacy policy.
