Privacy policy

Kalends is self-hosted software. It runs on your own computer or server, and the developer never receives your data.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Kalends is open-source software published by Back Bay Web Design at github.com/BackBayWebDesign/kalends. There is no Kalends website account and no Kalends cloud service.

What Kalends accesses

When you choose Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft in your Kalends settings, Kalends asks for:

  • Read-only access to your calendars (Google: calendar.readonly; Microsoft: Calendars.Read): the list of your calendars and their events' titles, times and locations.
  • Your email address and name (openid, email, profile): only to label the connected account in your settings.

Kalends cannot create, change or delete events, and does not access email, contacts, files or anything else in your account.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show your events on your own Kalends calendar display. It is fetched directly from Google or Microsoft by your Kalends server and is not sent to the developer or to any other party. The developer operates no servers that receive Kalends data.

Storage

Your Kalends server keeps the sign-in tokens and your settings in its own database on your machine, and keeps fetched events briefly in memory to refresh the display. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

Kalends does not sell, share, rent or transfer your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No humans read your data.

Kalends' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • In Kalends: Settings → Accounts → Remove. This deletes the account's tokens and calendars from your Kalends and, for Google, revokes Kalends' access.
  • In your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • In your Microsoft account: account.live.com/consent/Manage (personal accounts) or myapps.microsoft.com (work or school).

Deleting Kalends' data folder removes everything it stored.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
